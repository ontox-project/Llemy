**Drugs that Might Mimic the Effects of Rapamycin by Acting on Parallel or Downstream Pathways**

Rapamycin is a well-known inhibitor of mTORC1, a central regulator of cell growth, proliferation, and autophagy. To identify drugs that could mimic rapamycin’s effects, we focus on agents that target pathways downstream or parallel to mTORC1 signaling, particularly those influencing autophagy, cell cycle arrest, or metabolic regulation.

**Drivers Maintaining the Pathology**

The key drivers that sustain this pathological response include:

- **Pro-inflammatory cytokines:** IL-1, IL-6, TNF-α, IL-17, and IFN-γ, which activate NF-κB and MAPK pathways, creating a self-sustaining inflammatory loop.
- **Pattern recognition receptors:** TLR4, TLR5, TLR7, TLR8, TLR9, which detect environmental and microbial stimuli, perpetuating immune activation.
- **Transcription factors:** NF-κB subunits (RELA, NFKB1, REL, RELB, NFKB2) and STAT family members (STAT3, STAT6, STAT1, STAT4, STAT5A/B), which drive cytokine gene expression and immune cell proliferation.
- **Growth factors and cytokines:** TGF-β1, IL-13, IL-4, IL-5, which promote tissue remodeling, fibrosis, and eosinophil recruitment.

**Summary**

The triggers initiating this pathological response are primarily cytokine signaling (notably IL-1, IL-6, TNF-α, IFN-γ, IL-17, IL-4, IL-13) and pattern recognition receptor activation (TLRs). These activate downstream NF-κB and MAPK pathways, leading to cytokine production, immune cell recruitment, and tissue remodeling. Once established, the response is maintained by positive feedback loops involving cytokines, transcription factors, and immune cell interactions, with key drivers including cytokines like IL-6, IL-1, TNF-α, and growth factors such as TGF-β1.

**Downstream Changes Expected from Attenuation of Nampt Activity**

Nicotinamide phosphoribosyltransferase (Nampt) is a key enzyme in the NAD+ salvage pathway, catalyzing the conversion of nicotinamide to nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN), a precursor for NAD+ synthesis. Attenuation of Nampt activity would lead to decreased NAD+ levels, impacting numerous NAD+-dependent processes, including sirtuin activity, mitochondrial function, and cellular metabolism.

**Measurable Downstream Changes and Biomarkers**

1. **Reduced NAD+ Levels**: The most direct downstream effect of decreased Nampt activity is a reduction in cellular NAD+ concentrations. This can be measured using NAD+ quantification assays in tissues or cell extracts.

2. **Decreased Sirtuin Activity**: Sirtuins (e.g., SIRT1, SIRT3) are NAD+-dependent deacetylases involved in regulating metabolism, stress responses, and aging. Reduced NAD+ would impair their activity, leading to increased acetylation of their substrates. Biomarkers include:
- Increased acetylation levels of SIRT1/SIRT3 targets (e.g., p53, FOXO, PGC-1α).
- Changes in expression of SIRT-regulated genes involved in mitochondrial biogenesis and stress responses.

3. **Altered Mitochondrial Function**: Mitochondrial biogenesis and respiration are NAD+-dependent. Expected changes include:
- Decreased expression of mitochondrial genes such as **TFAM**, **COX5A**, **COX6A1**, **SDHB**, and **MT-CO2** (Source: Minerva Map Data, reactions involving mitochondrial biogenesis and respiration).
- Reduced mitochondrial DNA transcription and replication activity.

4. **Increased Oxidative Stress**: NAD+ depletion impairs mitochondrial function, leading to increased reactive oxygen species (ROS) production. Biomarkers include:
- Elevated levels of **superoxide** and **hydrogen peroxide** (Source: Reactions involving superoxide and hydrogen peroxide formation).
- Increased expression of oxidative stress response genes such as **HMOX1** and **PRDX3**.

5. **Altered Energy Metabolism**: NAD+ is essential for glycolysis and TCA cycle activity. Expected changes:
- Decreased activity of glycolytic enzymes like **GAPDH** and **PKM2**.
- Reduced TCA cycle flux, evidenced by decreased levels of intermediates like **succinate** and **pyruvate**.

6. **Impaired DNA Repair and Chromatin Remodeling**: NAD+ is a substrate for PARP enzymes involved in DNA repair. Reduced NAD+ may lead to:
- Decreased PARP activity, resulting in accumulation of DNA damage markers.
- Altered chromatin states, with changes in histone acetylation and methylation, detectable via histone modification biomarkers (e.g., acetylated histones, methylated histones).

7. **Cellular Senescence and Aging Markers**: NAD+ decline is associated with cellular aging:
